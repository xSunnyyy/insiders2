How the score works

Each signal is normalised to 0–100 and weighted: sentiment 35%, mention momentum vs 24h ago 20%, 1-month trend + 50-day average 20%, recent insider buying 15%, today's price move 10%. Signals we couldn't fetch are dropped and the rest re-weighted. Hover a score to see its parts. It's a transparent heuristic, not a forecast — not financial advice.
